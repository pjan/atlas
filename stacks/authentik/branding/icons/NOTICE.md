# Application icons

The icons of the authentik application dashboard (README "Dashboard"), served
at `/static/dist/custom/icons/`. Each file is named after its application's
slug. All product names, logos, and trademarks are the property of their
respective owners; they are used here only to identify the applications, and
no endorsement is implied.

Apart from resizing houndarr.png (below), the files are not edited. On the
dark page, `brand.yaml` adjusts three of them with CSS filters: backrest.svg
(inverted), radarr.svg (light outline), and komodo.svg (brightened).
